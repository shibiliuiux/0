TRACK MY SPEND

UX Design Case Study

Project Overview

Track My Spend is an app that helps people manage their finances. It shows users where their money goes each month. Apps, like Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm let users see their transaction history. They do not give a clear idea of how much is spent on different things. This project helps users track their expenses automatically. It also helps them understand their spending habits. The goal is to help users make financial decisions. It gives users insights into their spending. Suggests ways to improve. My job was to find out the challenges people face while spending. I had to identify the problems users face and design a way for them to track their expenses.

Core Strategy

Problems

  1. Spending Awareness Gap – People can see transactions but cannot easily understand where most of their money is spent.
  2. No Category-Based Tracking – It's hard to figure out what people spending on things, like: Food, Shopping, Bills, Transport, Others.
  3. Over Spending Without Realization – People often exceed budgets before realizing it.
  4. Poor Saving Habits – If people don't know where their money is going, they miss opportunities to save.

Design Process

  1. Research – I start by trying to get an understanding of how people spend their money and what financial challenges they face.
  2. User Interviews – Next I talk to people who actively use UPI to gather insights, from their experiences.
  3. Wireframing – Then I create layouts of the screens to visualize the apps structure.
  4. UI Design – After that I design interfaces that are easy to use and look clean.
  5. Prototyping – Finally I build interactive flows for testing.

Solution

Smart UPI-Based Expense Tracking

  1. Connect UPI Apps – Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Amazon Pay. The system automatically sorts users expenses into categories: Food, Shopping, Travel, Bills, Healthcare, Entertainment.
  2. Monthly Spending Dashboard – Users can see how much they spend each month with Monthly Spending Dashboard, It provides a picture of their expenses.
  3. Visual spending reports – This help users understand where their money is being spent
  4. Budget Monitoring – Track users spending limits with budget monitoring, helping them avoid overspending.
  5. Behaviour Insights – It shows users how their spending habits affect their savings
  6. Personalized Saving Suggestions – These suggestions help users find ways to cut down on expenses

User Research & Goals

Target Audience

  1. Salaried Employees – They want to manage their income and expenses better.
  2. College Students – They want to handle their pocket money in a way.
  3. Families – They want to see where their household spending goes
  4. Freelancers & Self-Employed Users – They need an easy way to track their finances without using complicated accounting tools

Goals

  • Increase spending awareness
  • Simplify expense tracking
  • Promote healthy saving habits
  • Help users make better financial decisions

User Research Insights

  • Method: Online Research (Reviewed: Expense tracking apps, Personal finance articles, User reviews) and Informal Interviews (Interviewed 5–8 friends and family members who use UPI).
  • Participants:
    • 3 Salaried Employees (They struggle to save money every month).
    • 2 College Students (They manage their allowances).
    • 2 Family Budget Managers (They track household expenses).
  • Insights:
    • Most people know how much they spend, but they don't know where they spend it.
    • People rarely look at their transaction history.
    • Food delivery and online shopping are major spending categories.
    • People want insights, not complex financial reports.

Overview

Key Features

The Track My Spend app focuses on solving real financial awareness problems with simple, automatic features:

  • Automatic UPI Category Sorting: Connects to UPI applications and categorizes expenses (Food, Shopping, Travel, Bills, Healthcare, Entertainment) automatically.
  • Monthly Spending Dashboard: Real-time visual spending charts that provide a clean picture of user expenditures.
  • Budget Monitoring & Alerts: Visual progress tracking of limits, helping users prevent overspending.
  • Personalized Saving Suggestions: Smart behavior insights to help users find actionable ways to save.

Usability Testing

Usability testing was conducted with a small group of regular UPI users representing the target personas. Feedback highlighted that:

  • Users prefer automatic categorization over manual input, as it encourages users to keep using the app regularly.
  • Users noticed their spending more often because of the alerts, the alerts helped them stay aware of their expenses.
  • Users found salary-based insights helpful, the insights helped them better understand their spending habits.
  • Users felt confident connecting their UPI accounts because the connection was secure and only transaction data was accessed.

Learnings

This app gave me valuable user experience lessons:

  • It became clear that users are more interested in understanding their spending than tracking transactions.
  • it showed that users prefer insights over raw data.
  • Recognized that tracking micro-transactions (small frequent spending) is the most critical feature for students and younger salaried workers.
  • It shows that behavior-based designs lead to stronger user engagement.
  • it showed that visual analytics help users make better decisions.

Final Outcome

Track my spend transforms transaction history into meaningful financial insights. By combining UPI integration, automatic categorization, budget tracking, and behavior-drive recommendations, the app helps users build healthier spending habits and make smarter financial decisions.
