CYBER SAFE

UX Design Case Study

Project Overview

CyberSafe is a digital platform aimed at raising awareness about cybercrimes and supporting victims through both technical and emotional guidance. The website is designed with four key sections: a Homepage introducing the mission, an Awareness Page with articles and videos, a Service Page offering access to ethical hackers and psychologists, and an Appointment Page for booking sessions.

Core Strategy

Problems

  1. Low Awareness – People don't understand common cybercrimes.
  2. No Guidance – Victims struggle to find help or report cases.
  3. Emotional Stress – Cybercrimes cause anxiety and loss of confidence.
  4. No Expert Access – Hard to reach ethical hackers or cybersecurity experts.
  5. Poor Post-Support – Victims lack counseling after incidents.

Design Process

  1. Research – Studied user problems and cybercrime challenges.
  2. Define – Framed problem statements and set clear goals.
  3. Ideate – Sketched layouts and user flows for four key pages.
  4. Design – Created wireframes and UI mockups.

Solution

  1. Awareness Content – Articles and videos to educate users about cybercrimes.
  2. Expert Access – Direct connection with ethical hackers for technical support.
  3. Appointment System – Simple booking to schedule sessions with experts.
  4. Emotional Support – Counseling from psychologists to help victims recover.

User Research & Goals

Target Audience

  1. General Users – People unaware of cyber threats.
  2. Victims – Those affected by scams or online fraud.
  3. Students & Young Adults – High-risk, active internet users.
  4. Families – Parents and elders needing online safety.

Goals

  • Increase cybercrime awareness.
  • Provide expert access and guidance.
  • Offer counseling support.
  • Enable simple appointment booking.

User Research Insights

  • Insight 1: Users are largely unaware of cyber threats.
  • Insight 2: Victims struggle to access experts quickly.
  • Insight 3: Cybercrime causes stress and emotional strain.
  • Needs Identified:
    • Learn about cyber risks.
    • Connect with cybersecurity experts.
    • Receive support for recovery and mental health.

Overview

Key Features

  • Awareness Hub – Easy-to-understand videos and articles on cyber safety.
  • Expert Access – Direct connection to ethical hackers for technical support.
  • Counseling Support – Psychologists available for emotional well-being after cybercrime.
  • Appointment System – Simple booking flow to schedule with experts.
  • Dual Navigation Paths – Separate entry for "Awareness" and "Services" from the homepage.
  • User-Friendly Design – Minimal, clear layout for quick understanding.

Usability Testing

  • No live testing conducted (concept stage).
  • Workflow checked for clear navigation and smooth booking flow.
  • Plan: future testing with users to validate awareness content and services.

Learnings

  • Importance of balancing awareness and services in one platform.
  • Clear flows make it easier for users to find help quickly.
  • Writing content in simple, non-technical language increases accessibility.
  • Realized that emotional support is as important as technical guidance.

Final Outcome

  • Designed a 4-page website with clear flows for both awareness and services.
  • Delivered educational content (videos, articles) and expert services (ethical hackers, psychologists).
  • Created a simple appointment system for easy access to professional help.
  • Final design ensures usability, clarity, and emotional support for cybercrime victims and learners.
